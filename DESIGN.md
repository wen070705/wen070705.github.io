# Design System

## Theme

An explorer's sketchbook rendered with loose brown-pencil contours, translucent watercolor fills, visible construction strokes, and generous paper space.

## Palette

All implementation colors use OKLCH: sky blue primary, leaf green structure, amber interaction, pure-white content surfaces, deep green ink.

## Typography

`ZCOOL XiaoWei` for short display phrases and `Noto Sans SC` for bilingual body text, with system fallbacks.

## Layout

Airy paper hero, oversized three-stop trail map, research clearing, and compact contact camp. Mobile becomes a linear illustrated expedition.

## Motion

Seasonal atmosphere, pointer firefly, depth shifts, and restrained node feedback. Disable nonessential motion with `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Components

Trail nav, season dial, quest nodes, detail dialog, research seed markers, and contact camp.
