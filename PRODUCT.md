# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students, researchers, collaborators, and recruiters discovering wenb through GitHub.

## Product Purpose

Create an interactive bilingual portfolio that turns wenb's research interests and projects into a gentle garden adventure, with clear paths to Visionary Grove, GitHub, and email.

## Brand Personality

Curious, gentle, quietly heroic.

## Anti-references

No pixel art, direct imitation of Stardew Valley, dark fantasy, neon cyberpunk dashboards, generic beige portfolios, dense badge walls, or gratuitous motion.

## Design Principles

1. Research feels explorable.
2. One mascot carries the story.
3. Interaction reveals meaning.
4. Every path has a clear destination.
5. Chinese and English belong together.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, readable contrast, alternative text, and reduced-motion support.
